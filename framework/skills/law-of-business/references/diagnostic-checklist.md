# Diagnostic Checklist

A pre-flight diagnostic to run against a real business, product, plan, pivot, price, or fundraise before the market runs it for you. It covers all 21 laws exactly once. Its job is not to console the founder but to produce a verdict: which laws the business violates, how badly, and what to do first.

## How to run it

1. Answer every check from **evidence**, not opinion. If the honest answer is "we believe" or "probably", it is not evidence.
2. Mark each check `yes`, `no`, or `unknown`.
3. Treat every `unknown` as `no`. An unmeasured risk is an unmanaged risk, and the source is full of companies that did not know until it was too late.
4. Every `no` is a red. Read the mapped reference for that law before deciding anything; the law tells you the mechanism, the checkbox only tells you that you have it.
5. Apply the scoring rule below, then report the reds by law ID with the evidence that produced them.

## Fast triage (seven questions)

Answer these first. Any single "no" means the business is not ready to scale, raise, or pivot on.

1. Is there a problem customers **pay** to solve today, and can I name them? → [`market-and-demand.md`](market-and-demand.md) (A1)
2. Is the reachable market big enough to carry the company's fixed costs without a "1%" assumption? → A2
3. Is the timing right — are the precondition, budget, and infrastructure in place now? → A3
4. Will customers pay more than it costs to serve them? → A4
5. Can I acquire customers repeatably through a channel I control? → A5
6. Why would a customer choose us over the cheaper, well-funded alternative? → A6
7. Do we have twelve-plus months of runway and unit economics that work on one unit? → [`capital-and-economics.md`](capital-and-economics.md) (B1, B2)

## Part A — Market & Demand

- **A1. Real Problem.** Do we have paying customers whose behavior proves the problem is real? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A2. Market Size.** Does a bottom-up market size clear our fixed costs at attack scale? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A3. Timing.** Is demand unblocked now, with a named leading indicator that it is arriving? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A4. Willingness to Pay.** Is our price above the fully-loaded cost to serve, proven by paying customers? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A5. Distribution.** Do we have one repeatable, owned acquisition channel with acceptable economics? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A6. Differentiation.** Can we name a structural advantage the incumbent cannot cheaply copy? → `market-and-demand.md`
- **A7. Right Customer.** Do we know the buyer, the budget they control, and the trigger, from direct interviews? → `market-and-demand.md`

## Part B — Capital & Economics

- **B1. Runway.** Do we hold twelve-plus months of runway with no uncommitted round as the next milestone? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B2. Unit Economics.** Is contribution margin positive on one unit before any scale assumption? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B3. External Dependence.** Is there a tested fallback for every partner, platform, or supplier that could end us? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B4. Mismatched Backer.** Do our backers' goals, timeline, and terms match the plan and permit a normal next round? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B5. Revenue Before Fundraising.** Are paying customers growing without a new round? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B6. Cost Discipline.** Is every fixed cost tied to a live metric and a written cut list? → `capital-and-economics.md`
- **B7. Downward Spiral.** Are all obligations current, with restructuring done from control rather than under a creditor? → `capital-and-economics.md`

## Part C — Execution & Operations

- **C1. Focus.** Is there one ideal customer, one problem, and one channel this quarter, with a written "no" list? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C2. Shipping.** Has a real user used the current build twice in the last month? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C3. Proof Before Scale.** Have we passed the retention and unit-economics gate before spending to expand? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C4. Operational Reality.** Does the operation reliably deliver its promise at today's volume, with improving cost per unit? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C5. The Team.** Is there one accountable leader, resolved founder roles, and no unresolved conduct or key-person risk? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C6. Regulatory Reality.** Have we mapped every rule, ruling, or regulator that could stop the core revenue line, and costed it in? → `execution-and-operations.md`
- **C7. Adaptation.** Are there explicit kill/iterate criteria for the current strategy, and capital left to act on them? → `execution-and-operations.md`

## Scoring

- **Automatic stop.** A red on **B1. Runway** or **A1. Real Problem** is an automatic stop. No other law can compensate: without runway the company cannot act, and without a real problem no amount of money or execution helps. Fix the red before any other move.
- **Stop and fix.** Three or more reds anywhere, or a red in both a Market and a Capital law, means the business is not ready to scale, raise, or pivot. Fix the market and capital reds first; they gate everything else.
- **Proceed with caution.** One or two reds: proceed only after writing the specific counter-move for each and a date to re-check it.
- **Green.** No reds, every `unknown` converted to evidence: the plan survives this diagnostic. Re-run it monthly and after any material change.
