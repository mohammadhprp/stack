# Case Index — The Evidence Base

This file is the categorized evidence base for the `law-of-business` skill. Every row is one real post-mortem from the source, filed under the single law it best illustrates. Use it to ground a diagnosis in named companies rather than generic advice: find the symptom, read the law, then read the cases here for the pattern.

**The grouping is this skill's interpretation, not the source's.** The source is a flat list of post-mortems with no law structure of its own; the A/B/C domains and the per-case filing below are the skill's distillation. A failure almost always violates several laws at once, so each case is filed once under its most instructive law — a judgement call, not a fact. Treat a row as evidence of the company and its cause (both reproduced from the PDF), and treat its placement as a reading you can move to a neighbouring law when the case's mechanism fits there better.

**Coverage.** All **432** titled entries in `442_Startup_Failure_Post_Mortems_1680124938.pdf` are captured below — one row per entry. The document is titled *442* but only **432** of its entries expose a machine-readable `Title:` block, and all 432 are captured here. Company names and source titles are reproduced from the PDF after normalizing ligatures and wrapped lines; the "What happened" column is a one-line cause drawn from that entry's body. Because a failure almost always violates several laws at once, each case is filed once, under its most instructive law, rather than repeated across every law it touches.

**How to read.** Columns are `Company | What happened | Source title`. The source title is the headline the post-mortem was published under, so it is the fastest way to trace a row back to the PDF. A case can move you to a neighbouring law: the cross-links inside each reference file point at those, and the `diagnostic-checklist.md` decides which law a live business actually violates.
